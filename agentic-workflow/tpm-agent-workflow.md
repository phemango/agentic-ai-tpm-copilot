# Agentic TPM Workflow

## 1. Purpose

This document defines the end-to-end workflow used by the Agentic AI TPM Copilot to support a human Technical Program Manager.

The workflow demonstrates how Agentic AI can move beyond simple question answering by maintaining context, performing multi-step reasoning and actions, checking results, and preparing structured outputs for human review.

---

# 2. Core Agentic Workflow

```text
Human TPM Request
        ↓
Understand
        ↓
Observe / Gather
        ↓
Plan
        ↓
Act
        ↓
Observe
        ↓
Analyze
        ↓
Verify
        ↓
Report
        ↓
Human Decision
